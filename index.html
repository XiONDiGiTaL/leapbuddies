  • Online — each player uses their own keyboard, and everyone uses the Player 1 keys.
  • Bump gold blocks for bananas · food restores stars or lives · three stars and three lives per run.
  • Both floor plates open the gate · stand on your buddy's head for a boost · pop their bubble to bring them back.
  • Stay close: fall too far behind and you are floated back to your partner in a bubble.
  • M mutes · a gamepad works for Player 1 · on a phone, use the touch pad under the stage.
